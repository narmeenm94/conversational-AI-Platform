# Helsinki XR Center

Source checked: 2026-07-17
Official sources: https://helsinkixrcenter.com/ and https://helsinkixrcenter.com/about/ and https://helsinkixrcenter.com/hub/

Helsinki XR Center, also called HXRC, is a leading European research, development, innovation, and community hub for emerging technologies, especially extended reality and artificial intelligence. It connects startups, industries, innovators, students, professionals, and investors for co-creation, new business, learning, experimentation, and talent development. HXRC opened in early 2019 and is operated by Metropolia University of Applied Sciences. It is located at Metropolia's Creative Campus Arabia, Hämeentie 135 A, on the third and fourth floors in Helsinki, Finland.

XR means extended reality and includes virtual reality, augmented reality, and mixed reality. HXRC supports Finnish XR startups, organizes XR and AI events, carries out collaborative research, development, and innovation projects, and works closely with Metropolia's XR Design degree. Its services include the HXRC Showroom, XR workshops, XR development, AI proof-of-concept services, startup services, and special facilities.

## HXRC Developer Hub

The Developer Hub is a program for early-stage companies working in spatial computing, the metaverse, XR, AI, Web3, and related technologies. It offers free workspace and hardware, mentoring, networking, co-learning, meeting rooms, events, and access to the HXRC Showroom. Member teams retain full ownership of their companies and products. The program begins with a three-month trial aimed at producing a demo, followed by renewable three-month continuation periods guided by a twelve-month roadmap.

The current Developer Hub teams listed by HXRC are 4D Haptics, ARchéProject, ARVO, Avatar's Journey, Birth of a Bird, Digiole, HappiByte, Helsinki Blockchain Center, ikkio, LIFECHO, Lincoln School, Mandatory Trainings, Manevski Studio, Mixx Reality, Nerphy, NFBT, Parallel Universe, Starklass, SoundxVision, T.A.R.S.I.E.R., Trialroom, VOIMA mobility, VReal, VR Studio, Willow, and Wright & Co.
